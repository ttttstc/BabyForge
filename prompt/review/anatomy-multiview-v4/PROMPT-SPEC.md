# BabyForge anatomy multiview V4

## Shared visual contract

- Scientific-educational 3D teaching model, not a decorative illustration.
- One identical organ assembly shown as a true turntable in four equal panels: front, anterior-left 45°, posterior-right 45°, back.
- Pure white background, centered subject, identical scale and vertical orientation in every panel.
- Smooth high-quality PBR surfaces, rounded educational-model finish, clean softbox lighting and crisp contact shadow.
- Vivid medically plausible palette: saturated coral and rose soft tissue, warm ivory bone and teeth, clear red arteries, cobalt-blue veins, golden-yellow nerves/fat and restrained sage-green secondary structures.
- Stronger saturation and contrast than the ear V3 model while avoiding neon color, gore, blood, wet tissue, horror, watercolor, flowers, labels, arrows, captions, bases or props.
- The four panels must not mirror, duplicate, add, remove, deform or recolor anatomy. Left/right, front/back and all counts must remain invariant.

## Anatomy locks

### Nose and sinuses

One complete symmetric nasal and paranasal-sinus teaching assembly. Preserve one septum, paired nasal cavities with three turbinates per side, paired maxillary and frontal sinuses, ethmoid air cells and one sphenoid-sinus region. Use natural pink mucosa and restrained fine red/blue vessels plus golden sensory-nerve branches where visible. No second nose, displaced septum or duplicated turbinates.

### Throat and larynx

One continuous pharynx-larynx assembly with nasopharynx, oropharynx, laryngopharynx, one epiglottis, one larynx with paired vocal folds, one upper trachea and one posterior esophagus. The trachea is anterior and the esophagus posterior. Add restrained red/blue laryngeal vessels and golden laryngeal-nerve branches without obscuring landmarks. No duplicated tubes or epiglottis.

### Mouth and primary teeth

One simplified teaching assembly representing a typical approximately 12-month-old dentition. Show only the upper and lower gingival arches plus exactly eight erupted primary incisors: four upper and four lower. Posterior gingival ridges remain toothless. Upper and lower arches remain at the same gentle open angle in all views. No lips, tongue, palate, cheeks, facial skin, jaw bone, exposed roots, vessels, nerves, canines, molars, premolars, permanent teeth or extra tooth rows. This is a representative educational snapshot; real eruption timing varies normally between children.

### Stomach and esophagus

One continuous esophagus-stomach assembly with one esophagus entering the cardia, fundus superior-left, body, lesser and greater curvature, antrum, pylorus and a short proximal duodenum. Add restrained gastric arteries/veins along the curvatures and golden anterior/posterior vagal branches without obscuring the silhouette or fixed rugae window. No duplicated stomach, outlet or esophagus; no intestine loops.

### Bladder and lower urinary tract

One neutral pediatric urinary-bladder assembly with two distal ureters entering the posterior bladder and one single midline urethral outlet inferiorly. Smooth detrusor wall and trigone visible in a restrained cutaway. Add restrained paired vesical vessels and golden pelvic autonomic nerve branches without implying external genital anatomy. No kidneys, external genitalia, duplicated urethra or extra ureters.

### Pediatric long bone

One pediatric long-bone teaching assembly, femur-like silhouette with proximal and distal epiphyses, metaphyses, a clearly continuous growth plate at each end, diaphysis, cortical shell, cancellous bone and medullary cavity. Add one nutrient artery/vein entering the mid-diaphysis, restrained metaphyseal/epiphyseal vessels that do not cross the growth plates, and sparse golden periosteal nerves. Use a single restrained longitudinal cutaway; no fracture, deformity, duplicated bone or adult-closed growth plates.
